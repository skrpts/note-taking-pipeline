## Capability

Reads a paper, chapter, or article and pulls out its key arguments, the evidence offered for each, the author's position, and the terminology a student needs to carry forward. Keeps the citation attached so later synthesis can attribute claims.

## When to Use

- Working through a reading list where each item must yield usable notes
- Separating what a source argues from what it merely cites
- Building a term glossary alongside the notes

## Inputs

Reading content, citation, text type

## Outputs

Structured reading notes covering key arguments, supporting evidence, author position, terminology, and the citation
