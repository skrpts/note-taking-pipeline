## Capability

Draws together everything captured during a study week — structured lecture notes, reading
extractions and concept maps — into a single summary that says what the week actually established.
Surfaces recurring themes, questions left unresolved, and topics where coverage is thin.

## What It Does

- consolidates the week's notes and maps into one document
- names the themes that recur across several sources
- lists unresolved questions rather than smoothing over them
- identifies topics needing further study before revision

## When to Use

- at the end of a study week, or before a revision session
- when several sources need to be seen together rather than one at a time

## Inputs

The week's structured notes, reading extractions and concept maps.

## Outputs

A weekly synthesis document covering themes, open questions and areas needing more work.
